Most entertaining sports, Soccer

Soccer is one of the world's most popular and entertaining sports. It is played, and watched by billions of people worldwide. Soccer is mainly played on the field by two teams. Each of the two teams consists of 11 players, and their coach. Soccer fans gather around to support their teams play at their home stadiums.

A soccer game is played in 90 minutes, excluding additional extra time. There are 10 positions in soccer: goalkeeper, defenders, midfielders, and forwards, and 3 officials. Some rules in soccer are offsides, fouls, corner kicks, free kicks, penalty, and throw-ins. There are different leagues, and divisions in soccer worldwide. One of the most popular competitions is the FIFA World Cup, which is held every four years.
