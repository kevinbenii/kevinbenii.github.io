The story behind K·AI

As a college student, I've seen firsthand how much AI has changed daily life — not just for me, but for everyone around me. It's gone from a novelty to something people rely on every day, for work, school, and everything in between.

That's why I built K·AI, named after myself. Instead of AI tools being scattered across a hundred different sites, K·AI brings them together in one place — organized by what they're actually good for, from education and research to coding, business, and creative work.

My goal is simple: make K·AI a central AI hub where anyone can explore the different types of AI tools out there and find the ones that fit what they need.

“My main goal for K·AI is to be a central AI hub for users to explore all different types of artificial intelligence and tools for their needs.”

— Kevin Beni, Creator of K·AI
