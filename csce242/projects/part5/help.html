Frequently asked questions

Can't find what you're looking for? Reach out below.

What is K·AI?

K·AI is a central hub for discovering AI tools across education, coding, business, and creative work, so you don't have to search for them one at a time.

How are tools chosen for the directory?

Every tool on K·AI is picked and organized by hand into one of four categories, rather than being pulled automatically from a list.

Is K·AI free to use?

Browsing the K·AI directory is free. Individual tools may have their own pricing once you open them.

How do I find a tool for a specific task?

Start on the AI Directory page and pick the category that matches what you're trying to do, or open a category directly from the homepage.

Can I suggest a tool to add?

Not yet through the site itself, but that feature is planned for a future update.
