THE STORY

From a personal list to a full directory

K·AI started as a personal project by Kevin Beni, a college student who noticed how much AI was changing daily life — and how scattered the tools for it were. What began as a way to organize a growing list of favorites turned into a central hub for education, coding, business, and creative tools alike, built for students first.
