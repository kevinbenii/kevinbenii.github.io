Study

Turns your notes into a study guide.

Open Study Back to Education and Research

Study takes whatever you already have — lecture notes, slides, or a textbook chapter — and turns it into an organized study guide, complete with a summary, key terms, and practice questions.

Students often lose time retyping the same material into flashcards or outlines by hand. Study handles that step automatically, so more of your study session goes toward actually learning the material instead of formatting it.
